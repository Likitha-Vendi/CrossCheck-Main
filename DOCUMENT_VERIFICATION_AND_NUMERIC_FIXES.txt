CrossCheck update

1. Numeric inputs
- Experience, notice period, current salary, expected salary, candidate interest, interview feedback and previous acceptance rate can be cleared fully.
- Zero is no longer forced back into an empty field.
- Values are converted to numbers only when the form is submitted.

2. Backend document storage
- Uploaded PDF/JPG/PNG/WEBP files are stored in the database and in the configured data/uploads directory.
- Identity and offer-letter files submitted from Add/Edit Candidate are synchronized into the documents table.
- Files remain available after refresh and backend restart.

3. Document review
- Each document starts as Pending.
- HR/Admin can View, Verify, Reject or return it to Pending.
- A document cannot be verified when its stored content is missing.

4. Trust analytics
- Trust points are awarded only after the matching employment, education, identity, offer-letter and required document checks are Verified.
- Missing, pending, rejected, failed or mismatched evidence receives no verification points.
- Risk score, trust score, overall verification status and AI recommendation recalculate after every review.
